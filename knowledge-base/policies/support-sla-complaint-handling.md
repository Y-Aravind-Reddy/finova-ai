# Customer Support SLA and Complaint Handling

Apex Global Bank - Customer Service
Document ID: PRC-SUP-005 | Version 2.2 | Owner: Head of Customer Experience
Illustrative policy for the Finova demo workspace. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets response and resolution targets for customer support tickets and the escalation path for complaints.

## 2. Ticket Priorities and SLA Targets

| Priority | Examples | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Card fraud, account blocked, failed salary credit | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| High | Failed transfer, wrong debit, KYC rejection | 1 hour | 24 hours |
| Medium | Statement request, limit change, address update | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| Low | General inquiry, product information | 1 business day | 5 business days |

The default SLA target for an unclassified ticket is 4 hours.

## 3. Ticket Statuses

- Open: received, not yet picked up.
- In Progress: assigned to a support executive.
- Pending Client: waiting for information from the customer. The SLA clock pauses in this status.
- Resolved: solution provided; customer has 7 days to reopen.
- Closed: resolved and confirmed, or auto-closed after 7 days.

## 4. Escalation Matrix

1. Level 1 - Support Executive: handles the ticket.
2. Level 2 - Team Lead: takes over automatically if the resolution target is breached.
3. Level 3 - Nodal Officer: handles complaints unresolved after 15 days or escalated by the customer.
4. If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the customer may approach the Banking Ombudsman.

## 5. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

After every resolved ticket the customer is invited to rate the service from 1 to 5. A rating of 2 or below triggers a callback from a Team Lead within 2 business days. The CSAT target is an average of 4.2 or higher.

## 6. Service Conduct

- Always verify identity before discussing account details.
- Never ask for full card numbers, PINs, CVV or OTPs.
- Never disclose fraud or AML investigation details; use "Your transaction is under routine review."
