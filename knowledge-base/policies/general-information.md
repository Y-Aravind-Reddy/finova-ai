# General Information for All Users

Apex Global Bank on the Finova Platform
Document ID: GEN-INF-001 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Customer Experience
Illustrative information for the Finova demo workspace. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. About Finova

Finova is the digital banking and operations platform used by Apex Global Bank. Customers use it to view accounts, cards, transactions and support tickets. Bank staff use it for operations dashboards, fraud monitoring, support and compliance.

## 2. Using the Website

- Sign in with your registered email and password. Your role decides which pages and data you can see.
- The left sidebar lists the pages available to your role.
- The Finova AI Assistant answers questions about the topics allowed for your role, using live data and the bank's policy documents.
- Use the date filter (30D, 90D, YTD, All History) at the top of dashboards to change the reporting period.
- Sign out after every session on a shared device.

## 3. Contact and Service Hours

- Customer care: available 24x7 through the Support page in the Finova app.
- Branch banking hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:30 to 16:30 IST, except second and fourth Saturdays and public holidays.
- Lost or stolen card: block it immediately from the Cards page, then raise an Urgent support ticket.

## 4. Documents Needed to Open an Account

- PAN card (mandatory).
- One proof of identity: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence.
- One proof of address: Aadhaar, passport, a utility bill from the last 3 months, or a registered rent agreement.
- A recent photograph and a video-KYC or in-person verification.

## 5. Reporting an Unauthorised Transaction

- Report it immediately through Support or by blocking the card in the app.
- If you report within 3 working days, your liability is zero once the claim is validated.
- The disputed amount is usually credited back within 10 working days while the review continues.

## 6. Staying Safe

- The bank will never ask for your PIN, CVV, OTP or full card number by phone, email or chat.
- Only use the official Finova app or website.
- If a transaction is held, you will see the message "Your transaction is under routine review."
