# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Escalation Policy

Apex Global Bank - Financial Crime Compliance
Document ID: POL-AML-002 | Version 4.0 | Owner: Chief Compliance Officer
Illustrative policy for the Finova demo workspace. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the mandatory thresholds and timelines for escalating suspicious activity detected by transaction monitoring, staff observation, or customer reports.

## 2. Mandatory Escalation Thresholds

An alert must be escalated to the AML Investigations team when any of the following occur:

1. A single cash deposit or withdrawal of INR 10,00,000 or more.
2. Multiple cash transactions that together reach INR 10,00,000 or more within one calendar month (structuring).
3. A cross-border transfer of USD 10,000 equivalent or more to or from a high-risk jurisdiction.
4. A transaction monitoring risk score of 80 or above. These cases are also auto-frozen pending review.
5. A risk score between 50 and 79 combined with any adverse media match or politically exposed person (PEP) flag.
6. Rapid movement of funds: more than 80 percent of a credit moved out within 48 hours with no clear business purpose.
7. Transactions inconsistent with the customer's declared income or business profile by more than 3 times.

## 3. Escalation Timeline

- Level 1 (Analyst): reviews the alert within 24 hours and documents the rationale for closing or escalating.
- Level 2 (AML Investigations): completes the investigation within 7 calendar days of escalation.
- Level 3 (Principal Officer): decides whether to file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR). An STR must be filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit within 7 days of the suspicion being confirmed.

## 4. Account Freeze Rules

- Risk score of 80 or above: debits are frozen automatically (status "Frozen Escrow") until Level 2 clears or confirms the case.
- A freeze longer than 30 days requires written approval from the Principal Officer.
- Credits may continue to post to a frozen account unless law enforcement instructs otherwise.

## 5. Tipping-Off Prohibition

Staff must never tell a customer that an alert, investigation or STR exists. Customer-facing teams use the neutral wording: "Your transaction is under routine review."

## 6. Record Keeping

All alerts, investigation notes and STR decisions are retained for 10 years. Closed alerts must contain a written rationale; "no issue found" alone is not acceptable.

## 7. Roles and Responsibilities

- Finance Analyst / AML Analyst: Level 1 review and documentation.
- Admin: ensures alert queues are staffed and SLA breaches are escalated.
- Principal Officer: STR decision and regulator communication.
