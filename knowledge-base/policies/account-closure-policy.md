# Account Closure Policy

Apex Global Bank - Retail and Institutional Banking
Document ID: POL-ACC-004 | Version 3.1 | Owner: Operations Control
Illustrative policy for the Finova demo workspace. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how customer accounts are closed, whether the closure is requested by the customer or initiated by the bank, so that balances are settled correctly, records are retained, and no closure is used to hide suspicious activity.

## 2. Customer-Requested Closure

1. The customer submits a closure request through net banking, the mobile app, or a signed branch form.
2. Support verifies identity using two factors: registered mobile OTP plus one of date of birth, PAN, or the last transaction amount.
3. The account must have no pending debits, no open disputes, no active standing instructions, and no linked loan or overdraft balance.
4. Any remaining credit balance is transferred to a verified account in the customer's own name. Cash payout is allowed only up to INR 50,000 at a branch.
5. Cheque books and debit cards are cancelled in the core system on the same day.
6. The customer receives a closure confirmation letter within 2 business days.

Standard turnaround: closure is completed within 5 business days of a complete request.

## 3. Bank-Initiated Closure

The bank may close an account when:

- KYC remains incomplete 90 days after the re-KYC due date, after three written reminders.
- The account is dormant for more than 24 months with a zero balance.
- The account is linked to confirmed fraud or a regulatory directive.

A bank-initiated closure requires approval from a Branch Manager and the Compliance Officer. The customer receives at least 30 days' written notice, except where a regulatory or law-enforcement directive requires immediate action.

## 4. Restrictions

- An account under an open fraud case, AML review, or legal hold must not be closed. The closure request is parked until Compliance releases it.
- Accounts with a risk score of 80 or above must be reviewed by the Fraud Operations team before closure.
- Joint accounts require consent from all holders.

## 5. Record Retention

Account opening documents, KYC records and transaction history are retained for 10 years after the closure date. Closed accounts are flagged read-only and cannot be reactivated; a returning customer must open a new account with fresh KYC.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

- Support Executive: verifies identity, checks closure prerequisites, raises the request.
- Operations: settles balances, cancels instruments, closes the account in core banking.
- Compliance: approves bank-initiated closures and releases accounts on hold.
