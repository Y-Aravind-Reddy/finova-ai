# KYC Document Requirements

Apex Global Bank - Customer Onboarding
Document ID: POL-KYC-001 | Version 5.2 | Owner: Onboarding and Compliance
Illustrative policy for the Finova demo workspace. Not legal or regulatory advice.

## 1. Purpose

This policy lists the documents required to verify the identity and address of every customer before an account is opened, and the schedule for periodic re-verification (re-KYC).

## 2. Individual Customers

One proof of identity AND one proof of address are required.

Accepted proof of identity:
- PAN card (mandatory for all accounts)
- Aadhaar card (with masked number) or e-KYC via Aadhaar OTP
- Passport
- Voter ID card
- Driving licence

Accepted proof of address (must be dated within the last 3 months if a utility bill):
- Aadhaar card or passport showing current address
- Utility bill (electricity, water, gas, landline)
- Registered rent agreement

A recent photograph and a live video-KYC session or in-person verification complete the process.

## 3. Institutional and Corporate Customers

Institutional accounts require all of the following:

1. Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum and Articles of Association.
2. Company PAN and GST registration certificate.
3. Board resolution authorising the account opening and naming authorised signatories.
4. KYC documents (Section 2) for every authorised signatory.
5. Beneficial ownership declaration identifying every person holding 10 percent or more ownership or control.
6. Audited financial statements for the last 2 years.

## 4. Risk Tiers and Re-KYC Schedule

- Low risk (salaried individuals, standard tier): re-KYC every 10 years.
- Medium risk: re-KYC every 8 years.
- High risk (PEPs, high-value private wealth tiers, cross-border businesses): re-KYC every 2 years, with enhanced due diligence including source of funds.

## 5. Verification Status

- Pending: documents submitted, awaiting verification (target 2 business days).
- Under Review: discrepancy found; customer contacted for clarification.
- Verified: all checks passed.
- Rejected: documents invalid or forged. Forged documents are reported to Compliance.

Accounts with KYC still Pending after 30 days are restricted to credits only.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

- Support Executive: collects documents and explains requirements to the customer.
- Onboarding Officer: verifies documents and assigns the risk tier.
- Compliance: approves high-risk customers and enhanced due diligence.
